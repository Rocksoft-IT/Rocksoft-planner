**What this delivers:** The Timeline can now be sorted by contract type, not just by
name. A new "Name" / "Contract type" switch next to the existing filters lets you
group people UoP, then B2B, then Freelance, then unset, alphabetically within each
group. Switching back to "Name" restores today's order, and all existing filters
(people, project, skills, role) still work together with either sort.
